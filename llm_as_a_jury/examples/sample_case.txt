FICTIONAL CLASSROOM EXERCISE

Charge: Taking a library tablet without permission.
Classroom decision rule: Vote guilty only if the stated facts support that the
person knowingly took the tablet without permission. This rule is invented for
this exercise and is not legal advice or a real jurisdiction's law.

Facts:
- A tablet was missing from the library desk after closing.
- A camera recording shows Riley carrying a tablet-shaped object toward the exit.
- The recording is too blurry to identify the object.
- Riley says they returned a borrowed tablet to the front desk before leaving.
- The front desk log has no entry for a tablet that evening.
- No one saw Riley take the missing tablet from the desk.
